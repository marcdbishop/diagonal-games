The team

Every member of the Diagonal Games team is an AI agent. One human founder directs them, sets the plan and has the final say. Each agent owns one job, such as battles, world, story, art or testing, and the work gets reviewed before it goes in. We think you should know that when you look at our games.

  • DirectorKeeps the plan, makes the day-to-day calls and keeps the team pulling the same way. The founder can overrule any call.
  • Battle EngineerBuilds the battle engine: the timeline, timed reactions, Energy, roles, status effects, enemy AI, cards in battle and capture.
  • Combat DesignerDesigns how battles feel and balance: moves, abilities and how the roles work together.
  • Creature DesignerDesigns the Pawns: their lines, elements, roles and how they grow.
  • World DesignerDesigns the places you travel through: routes, towns and the map.
  • Narrative DesignerWrites the story, characters and dialogue.
  • Audio DesignerLooks after the music and sound.
  • QA EngineerTests the game and hunts down bugs before players find them.
  • ReviewerChecks the team's work before it is accepted into the game.
  • Community ManagerTalks with players and passes their feedback back to the team.
  • Code Health EngineerKeeps the codebase tidy and easy to change.
  • PlaytesterPlays the game as a player would and reports how it feels.
  • Legal & IP ReviewerWatches legal and IP questions so the studio stays on solid ground.
  • Finance OfficerLooks after the business side of the studio.
  • Web DeveloperBuilds and writes this website.
  • UI EngineerBuilds the in-game HUD, title screen and shared UI theme, and makes them good to use.
  • Art DesignerOwns the look of the game and the art pipeline, keeping all art in the approved style and game-ready.
  • Systems EngineerOwns the data and progression layer: saves, levels and evolution, relics, ranks, economy, the Dex and the asset tooling.
  • World EngineerOwns the overworld: the map engine, movement, encounters, dialogue, NPCs, tutorials and the flow between scenes.
  • Menu UI EngineerBuilds the menus and data screens: pause, Team, Summary, Deck, Dex and Settings.
  • UI & World ArtistDraws the small, exact art: UI frames, icons, badges, card frames, world tiles and props.
