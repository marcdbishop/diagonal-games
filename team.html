The team

Every member of the team is an AI agent with one job, such as battle code, level design, writing, QA or art. One human founder directs them. The founder decides what gets built and can overrule any call the team makes. Nothing outside the design document gets built without the founder's approval.

  • DirectorKeeps the plan together, makes day-to-day calls and logs them so the founder can overrule them.
  • Battle EngineerBuilds the battle engine: the timeline, timed reactions, Energy, roles, status effects, enemy AI, cards in battle and capture.
  • Combat DesignerDesigns how battles play: abilities, roles and the balance between them.
  • Creature DesignerDesigns the Pawns and their evolution lines.
  • World DesignerDesigns the places you travel through: routes, towns and the tower.
  • Narrative DesignerWrites the story, the characters and their dialogue.
  • Audio DesignerLooks after the music and sound of the game.
  • QA EngineerTests the game, looks for bugs and checks that fixes hold.
  • ReviewerReads the team's work before it goes in and checks it against the plan.
  • Community ManagerTalks with players and passes on what they tell us.
  • Code Health EngineerKeeps the codebase tidy and easy to change.
  • PlaytesterPlays the game as a player would and reports how it feels.
  • Legal & IP ReviewerChecks the studio's work for legal and IP issues.
  • Finance OfficerKeeps the studio's books in order.
  • Web DeveloperBuilds this website and writes its words.
  • UI EngineerOwns the in-game HUD, title screen and shared UI theme, and makes them good to use.
  • Art DesignerOwns the look of the game and the art pipeline, from approved style to game-ready art.
  • Systems EngineerOwns the data and progression layer: saves, levels, evolution, relics, the economy and Dex tracking.
  • World EngineerOwns the overworld: maps, movement, encounters, dialogue and NPCs, and the flow between scenes.
  • Menu UI EngineerBuilds the menus and data screens: pause, Team, Summary, Deck, Dex and Settings.
  • UI & World ArtistDraws the small, exact art: UI frames, icons, card frames, world tiles and props.
