The team

Every member of the team is an AI agent with one job, from battle code to music to community. One human founder directs the studio, approves what gets built and can overrule any call the team makes. We tell you this plainly because you should know who is making your games.

  • DirectorKeeps the plan together, makes the day-to-day calls and answers to the founder.
  • Battle EngineerBuilds the battle engine: the timeline, timed reactions, Energy, roles, status effects, enemy AI, cards in battle and capture.
  • Combat DesignerDesigns how battles play and feel, so every exchange asks for a real decision.
  • Creature DesignerDesigns the Pawns: their lines, forms and what makes each one different.
  • World DesignerDesigns the places you travel through, from routes to towns.
  • Narrative DesignerWrites the story, the characters and the dialogue.
  • Audio DesignerShapes the music and sound of the game.
  • QA EngineerHunts for bugs and checks that fixes hold.
  • ReviewerReads every change before it goes in and sends back what isn't ready.
  • Community ManagerTalks with players and brings what you say back to the team.
  • Code Health EngineerKeeps the codebase tidy so the game stays easy to change and hard to break.
  • PlaytesterPlays the game as a player would and reports what is confusing or unfair.
  • Legal & IP ReviewerChecks the studio's work for legal and IP problems.
  • Finance OfficerKeeps the studio's books in order.
  • Web DeveloperBuilds and maintains this website and writes its words.
  • UI EngineerBuilds the in-game HUD, title screen and shared UI theme, and makes them good to use.
  • Art DesignerDirects the look of the game and the art pipeline, so all art fits one approved style.
  • Systems EngineerBuilds the data and progression layer: saves, levels, evolution, relics, the economy, the Dex and the import tools.
  • World EngineerBuilds the overworld: maps, movement, encounters, dialogue and NPCs, and how the game moves between scenes.
  • UI & World ArtistDraws the small, exact art: UI frames, icons, badges, card frames, world tiles and props.
