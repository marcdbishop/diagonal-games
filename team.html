The team

Everyone on the Diagonal Games team except the founder is an AI agent. Each agent has one job, such as battle code, creature design, story, audio or QA. The human founder sets the direction and decides what gets built. Nothing goes into a game without that approval.

  • DirectorKeeps the plan together, makes day-to-day calls, and brings the big decisions to the founder.
  • Battle EngineerBuilds the battle engine: the timeline, timed reactions, Energy, roles, status effects, enemy AI, cards in battle and capture.
  • Combat DesignerDesigns how fights play: abilities, roles, timing and balance.
  • Creature DesignerDesigns the Pawns: their lines, forms and how they grow.
  • World DesignerDesigns the places you travel through: routes, towns and how they connect.
  • Narrative DesignerWrites the story, the characters and their dialogue.
  • Audio DesignerPlans the music and sound that give the game its mood.
  • QA EngineerTests the game, hunts for bugs and checks that fixes hold.
  • ReviewerReads the team's work before it goes in and catches mistakes.
  • Community ManagerLooks after the players and the Discord, and passes on what you tell us.
  • Code Health EngineerKeeps the codebase tidy and easy to work in.
  • PlaytesterPlays the game as a player would and reports how it feels.
  • Legal & IP ReviewerChecks the studio's work for legal and IP problems.
  • Finance OfficerKeeps the studio's records and admin in order.
  • Web DeveloperBuilds and maintains this website and writes its words.
  • UI EngineerBuilds the in-game HUD, title screen and shared interface theme, and makes them good to use.
  • Art DesignerOwns the look of the game and directs the art, so everything fits the approved style.
  • Systems EngineerBuilds the data layer: Pawn data, saves, levels, evolution, relics, the Dex and the asset tooling.
  • World EngineerBuilds the overworld: maps, movement, encounters, dialogue, NPCs and tutorials.
  • Menu UI EngineerBuilds the menus and data screens, like Team, Deck, Dex and Settings.
  • UI & World ArtistDraws the small, exact art: UI frames, icons, card frames, world tiles and props.
