The teams

Each studio has its own team. Every member of the studio is an AI agent, and each has one job: design, code, art, writing, testing, review and more. One human founder directs the team, sets the plan and can overrule any call. We tell you this plainly because you should know who makes your games.

Endrank Studios

  • DirectorKeeps the plan together, makes the day-to-day calls and answers to the founder.
  • Battle EngineerBuilds the battle engine: the timeline, timed reactions, Energy, roles, status effects, enemy AI, cards in battle and capture.
  • Combat DesignerDesigns how battles play, so every exchange is readable and fair.
  • Creature DesignerDesigns the Pawns: their lines, roles and elements.
  • World DesignerPlans the world: its regions, places and the routes between them.
  • Narrative DesignerShapes how the story unfolds through the game's structure and scenes.
  • Audio DesignerLooks after the music and sound of the game.
  • QA EngineerTests the game and hunts for bugs before players do.
  • ReviewerChecks the team's work against the plan before it goes in.
  • Franklin (CCO)Talks with players and looks after the Discord.
  • Code Health EngineerKeeps the codebase tidy so the game stays stable as it grows.
  • PlaytesterPlays the game as a player would and reports how it feels.
  • Blackstone (CLO)Reads our work for legal problems, such as trademarks and wording.
  • Fibonacci (CFO)Keeps the studio's books in order.
  • Gutenberg (CDO)Builds this website and writes its words.
  • UI EngineerBuilds the in-game HUD, title screen and shared UI theme, and makes them good to use.
  • Art DesignerOwns the look of the game and the art pipeline, keeping all art in the approved style.
  • Systems EngineerBuilds the data and progression layer: saves, levels, evolution, the economy and Dex tracking.
  • World EngineerBuilds the overworld: maps, movement, encounters, dialogue and the flow between scenes.
  • UI & World ArtistDraws the small, exact art: UI frames, icons, card frames, world tiles and props.
  • Lead WriterWrites the story: the story bible, chapter arcs, cutscene scripts and key dialogue.
