The team

Every member of the studio is an AI agent with one job, from battle code to music to QA. One human founder sets the direction and decides what gets built. The agents do the work, and the founder can overrule any of their calls.

  • DirectorKeeps the studio pointed in one direction and logs the calls the founder can overrule.
  • Battle EngineerBuilds the battle engine: the timeline, timed reactions, Energy, roles, status effects, enemy AI, cards in battle and capture.
  • Combat DesignerDesigns how battles play and feel, so every exchange is readable and fair.
  • Creature DesignerDesigns the Pawns: their lines, forms and what makes each one different.
  • World DesignerDesigns the places you travel through and how they connect.
  • Narrative DesignerWrites the story and its characters.
  • Audio DesignerMakes the music and sound for the game.
  • QA EngineerTests the game and hunts for bugs before players find them.
  • ReviewerReads the team's work and checks it before it goes in.
  • Community ManagerLooks after the players and the Discord, and carries your feedback back to the team.
  • Code Health EngineerKeeps the codebase tidy and easy to change.
  • PlaytesterPlays the game as a player would and reports how it feels.
  • Legal & IP ReviewerChecks the studio's work for legal and IP problems.
  • Finance OfficerKeeps the studio's books in order.
  • Web DeveloperBuilds this website and writes its words.
  • UI EngineerBuilds the in-game HUD, title screen and shared UI theme, and makes them good to use.
  • Art DesignerDirects the game's art and the art pipeline, keeping it in the approved style.
  • Systems EngineerBuilds the data and progression layer: saves, levels, evolution, relics, the Dex and the economy.
  • World EngineerBuilds the overworld: maps, movement, encounters, dialogue and how scenes flow together.
  • Menu UI EngineerBuilds the menus and data screens, like Team, Deck, Dex and Settings.
  • UI & World ArtistDraws the small, exact art: UI frames, icons, card frames, world tiles and props.
