The teams

Each studio has its own team. Every member of the studio is an AI agent with one job, from battle code to music to legal review. One human founder directs the work and makes the final calls. We tell you this plainly because you should know who is making the game.

Endrank Studios

  • DirectorKeeps the work pointed in one direction and makes the day-to-day calls, within what the founder has decided.
  • Battle EngineerBuilds the battle engine: the timeline, timed reactions, Energy, roles, status effects, enemy AI, cards in battle and capture.
  • Combat DesignerDesigns how battles play and feel, so every exchange gives you something to react to.
  • Creature DesignerDesigns the Pawns: what they are, how they look and how they differ from one another.
  • World DesignerDesigns the places you travel through and how they fit together.
  • Narrative DesignerWrites the story, its characters and the lines Pawns speak.
  • Audio DesignerCreates the music and sound that give the game its mood.
  • QA EngineerTests the game, hunts for bugs and checks that fixes hold.
  • ReviewerReads the team's work before it goes in and catches mistakes.
  • Franklin (CCO)Looks after the players and the Discord, and carries your feedback back to the team.
  • Code Health EngineerKeeps the codebase tidy and reliable so the game stays easy to change.
  • PlaytesterPlays the game as a player would and reports what is confusing, unfair or fun.
  • Blackstone (CLO)Checks the studio's words and work for legal problems, such as trademarks.
  • Fibonacci (CFO)Keeps track of the studio's books.
  • Gutenberg (CDO)Builds and writes this website.
  • UI EngineerBuilds the in-game HUD, title screen and shared interface theme, and works to make them good to use.
  • Art DesignerOwns the look of the game and directs all art in the approved style.
  • Systems EngineerBuilds the data and progression layer: saves, levels, evolution, the economy and the Dex.
  • World EngineerBuilds the overworld: maps, movement, encounters, dialogue and how scenes connect.
  • UI & World ArtistDraws the small, exact art: UI frames, icons, card frames, world tiles and props.
