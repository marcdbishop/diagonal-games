The team

Every member of the studio team is an AI agent with a defined role, from design and engineering to art, audio, QA and community. One human founder directs the work, makes the final decisions and can overrule any of them. We say this plainly because you should know who is making the games you play.

  • DirectorKeeps the studio pointed in one direction, makes the day-to-day calls and answers to the founder.
  • Battle EngineerBuilds the battle engine: the timeline, timed reactions, Energy, roles, status effects, enemy AI, cards in battle and capture.
  • Combat DesignerDesigns how fights feel and balance: abilities, roles and the rhythm of each exchange.
  • Creature DesignerDesigns the Pawns: their lines, elements, roles and how they grow.
  • World DesignerPlans the places you travel through, from routes and towns to dungeons.
  • Narrative DesignerWrites the story, the characters and the dialogue.
  • Audio DesignerShapes the music and sound of the game.
  • QA EngineerHunts for bugs and checks that what we build works the way it should.
  • ReviewerReads the team's work with a critical eye before it goes into the game.
  • Community ManagerLooks after the Discord and the players, and passes your feedback to the team.
  • Code Health EngineerKeeps the codebase tidy and maintainable so the game stays easy to change.
  • PlaytesterPlays the game as a player would and reports what is confusing, unfair or dull.
  • Legal & IP ReviewerChecks that our names, words and assets are safe to use and are really ours.
  • Finance OfficerKeeps the studio's books and paperwork in order.
  • Web DeveloperBuilds and writes this website.
  • UI EngineerBuilds the in-game HUD, title screen and shared interface theme, and makes them good to use.
  • Art DesignerDirects the look of the game and the art pipeline, so everything stays in one style.
  • Systems EngineerBuilds the data and progression layer: saves, levels, evolution, relics, the economy and the Codex.
  • World EngineerBuilds the overworld: the map, movement, encounters, dialogue, NPCs and the flow between scenes.
  • UI & World ArtistDraws the small, exact art: interface frames, icons, card frames, world tiles and props.
