The team

Everyone on the team besides the founder is an AI agent. Each has a defined job, such as battle code, story, art, audio or testing. The founder sets the direction, decides what goes into the game and can overrule any call the team makes. Nothing gets built without the founder's approval.

  • DirectorKeeps the team pointed the same way and takes the big decisions to the founder.
  • Battle EngineerBuilds the battle engine: turn order, timed reactions, Energy, roles, enemy AI, cards and capture.
  • Combat DesignerDesigns how fights play out and tunes them so they feel fair.
  • Creature DesignerDesigns the Pawns: what they are, how they look and what they can do.
  • World DesignerPlans the places you'll travel through and how they connect.
  • Narrative DesignerWrites the story, the characters and the dialogue.
  • Audio DesignerShapes the music and sound effects.
  • QA EngineerHunts for bugs and checks that fixes hold.
  • ReviewerReads over the team's work before it's accepted.
  • Community ManagerLooks after the Discord and passes player feedback to the team.
  • Code Health EngineerKeeps the codebase tidy so it stays easy to change.
  • PlaytesterPlays the game and reports what's confusing, unfair or dull.
  • Legal & IP ReviewerChecks names, text and assets for legal and IP problems.
  • Finance OfficerKeeps the studio's records and admin in order.
  • Web DeveloperBuilds this website and writes its words.
  • UI EngineerBuilds the in-game HUD, the title screen and the shared UI look.
  • Art DesignerDirects the game's art and keeps it in one consistent style.
  • Systems EngineerBuilds the data side: Pawns, saves, levels, evolution and progression.
  • World EngineerBuilds the overworld: maps, movement, NPCs, dialogue and the flow between scenes.
  • Menu UI EngineerBuilds the menus: Team, Deck, Dex, Settings and more.
  • UI & World ArtistDraws the small, exact art: UI frames, icons, card frames and world tiles.
