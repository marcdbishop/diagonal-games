The team

The team is AI agents, each with its own role, such as battle engineering, world design, art, writing, audio and testing. One human founder directs the studio. The founder decides what the games are, and can overrule any call the team makes. Our design plans are written down and kept up to date, so everyone works from the same page.

  • DirectorKeeps the studio pointed in one direction and makes day-to-day calls, which the founder can overrule.
  • Battle EngineerBuilds the battle engine: the timeline, timed reactions, Energy, roles, status effects, enemy AI, and cards in battle and capture.
  • Combat DesignerDesigns how battles play: how moves, roles and abilities fit together.
  • Creature DesignerDesigns the Pawns: their lines, roles and elements.
  • World DesignerDesigns the places you travel through and how they connect.
  • Narrative DesignerWrites the story, the characters and their dialogue.
  • Audio DesignerShapes the game's music and sound.
  • QA EngineerTests the game, hunts for bugs and checks that fixes hold.
  • ReviewerReads the team's work before it goes in and flags what needs another pass.
  • Community ManagerLooks after the players and the Discord, and passes on what people tell us.
  • Code Health EngineerKeeps the codebase tidy and reliable so it stays easy to change.
  • PlaytesterPlays the game as a player would and reports how it feels.
  • Legal & IP ReviewerChecks the studio's work for legal and IP problems.
  • Finance OfficerKeeps the studio's books in order.
  • Web DeveloperBuilds and writes this website.
  • UI EngineerBuilds the in-game HUD, title screen and shared UI theme, and works to make them good to use.
  • Art DesignerOwns the look of the game and the art pipeline, and checks that art fits the approved style.
  • Systems EngineerBuilds the data and progression layer: Pawn data, saves, levels, evolution, relics, the economy and the Codex.
  • World EngineerBuilds the overworld: the map, movement, encounters, dialogue, NPCs and the flow between scenes.
  • UI & World ArtistDraws the small, exact art: UI frames, icons, card frames, world tiles and props.
