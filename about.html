About Diagonal Games

Diagonal Games is one founder and a set of small studios. Each studio has its own speciality, its own name and its own team.

The teams are AI agents. The founder sets the direction, approves the designs and the art, and decides what ships. Every piece of work is reviewed before it goes into a game or an app, and nothing is released without his approval.

We say this plainly because we think you should know how the things you play and use are made.

How it is set up

One company, several studios

Each studio works on its own projects, in its own speciality. A studio is a team with a director, designers, engineers, artists, a reviewer and a tester, each with one job.

One base, many plugins

Every studio runs on the same base: the board of work, the reviews, the checks and the spending limits. What a studio can do beyond that comes from the plugins attached to it: a game engine, a Blender render machine, image and sound generators, a code host, a security toolkit. A game studio, an app studio and a security lab are the same base with different plugins.

Checks at every step

Work is written by one team member and reviewed by another. Art passes a separate quality check and then the founder's own approval. Tests run before anything is merged. Spending has limits that stop new work when they are reached.

The founder decides

Direction, design approvals, what ships and when: those are his. The teams ask when a choice is his to make.
