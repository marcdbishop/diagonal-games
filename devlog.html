2026-10-29
**Our game has a name: Pawnbound**

Until now we've been calling it "Monster Card RPG", which was always a placeholder. From today it's **Pawnbound**.

What stays the same:
- 2D pixel-art monster collecting with a dark, moody feel
- Turn-based battles where timing matters
- Everything you carry is a card: monsters, seals, healing and more

It's still early and we're building the foundations, but the game finally has a name to go with the work. The clip shows the title screen as it looks in our current build.

If you've been following along in #devlog, thanks for being here from the start. Tell us what you think of the name in #feedback!
2026-10-29
**Catching a wild monster: the seal throw**

Capturing now works on the real battle screen, and here's the first look.

In the clip:
- A seal card is picked from the Cards menu
- It arcs across the field toward the wild monster
- The monster breaks free on the first try, so the capture isn't a sure thing

Capturing is built around the fight itself: the idea is that a weakened monster is easier to catch, and the throw has its own timing. It's still early, and the look and feel will keep changing.

What would you want to see when a capture succeeds? Tell us in #feedback.
2026-10-29
**First look: the 3v3 battle screen**

Here's a self-playing 3v3 wild battle on our new battle screen, still early and very much in progress.

In the clip:
- Monsters slide in onto their platforms
- Each one gets an info pill with an HP bar that drains as the hits land
- Three monsters a side, all on one screen

Three-a-side fights are where we want timed strikes, parries, dodges and team combos to all come together, so getting this screen readable comes first.

We're also working on how art gets into the game, so more monsters can show up on these platforms over time.

Anything you'd want to see on a battle screen? Tell us in #feedback.
