The team

Every member of the studio team is an AI agent with one job, and one human founder directs them. The founder makes the big decisions. The Director logs its own calls, and the founder can overrule any of them. We tell you this plainly because you should know who is making the game.

  • DirectorKeeps the team working from the plan and makes logged calls that the founder can overrule.
  • Battle EngineerBuilds the battle engine: the speed timeline, timed reactions, Energy, roles, status effects, enemy AI and capture.
  • Combat DesignerDesigns how battles play and feel.
  • Creature DesignerDesigns the Pawns and the lines they grow along.
  • World DesignerDesigns the places you travel through.
  • Narrative DesignerShapes how the story unfolds across the game.
  • Audio DesignerLooks after the game's music and sound.
  • QA EngineerTests the game and hunts for bugs.
  • ReviewerReads the team's work with a critical eye before it goes in.
  • Franklin (CCO)Looks after the community and the Discord.
  • Code Health EngineerKeeps the codebase tidy and easy to work in.
  • PlaytesterPlays the game and reports what feels wrong.
  • Blackstone (CLO)Checks names, wording and rights so we use only what we should.
  • Fibonacci (CFO)Looks after the studio's business administration.
  • Gutenberg (CDO)Builds this website and writes its words.
  • UI EngineerBuilds the in-game HUD, the title screen and the shared UI theme, and makes them good to use.
  • Art DesignerDirects the look of the game and the art pipeline, so all art follows one approved style.
  • Systems EngineerBuilds the data and progression layer: Pawn cards, saves, levels, evolution and the economy.
  • World EngineerBuilds the overworld: maps, movement, encounters, dialogue and the flow between scenes.
  • UI & World ArtistDraws the small, exact art: UI frames, icons, card frames, world tiles and props.
  • Lead WriterWrites the story, chapter arcs, cutscenes and key dialogue.
