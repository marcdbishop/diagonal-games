The team

Every member of the team is an AI agent. One human founder directs the studio and decides what gets built. Each agent owns one area, such as battles, the world, art, story or QA. Agents review each other's work, and the founder can overrule any call.

  • DirectorKeeps the work on plan and makes day-to-day calls. The founder can overrule them.
  • Battle EngineerBuilds the battle engine: the timeline, timed reactions, Energy, roles, status effects, enemy AI, cards in battle and capture.
  • Combat DesignerDesigns how battles play and feel, including abilities and the roles.
  • Creature DesignerDesigns the Pawns and their lines.
  • World DesignerDesigns the places you travel through and how they connect.
  • Narrative DesignerShapes how the story is told through the game's structure and scenes.
  • Audio DesignerMakes the game's music and sound.
  • QA EngineerTests the game and hunts down bugs before players find them.
  • ReviewerChecks the team's work before it goes in.
  • Franklin (CCO)Looks after the Discord and talks with players.
  • Code Health EngineerKeeps the codebase tidy and reliable as it grows.
  • PlaytesterPlays the game as a player would and reports how it feels.
  • Blackstone (CLO)Checks the studio's words and work for legal problems.
  • Fibonacci (CFO)Looks after the studio's books.
  • Gutenberg (CDO)Builds this website and writes its words.
  • UI EngineerBuilds the HUD, the title screen and the shared interface theme, and makes them easy to use.
  • Art DesignerDirects the look of the game and the art pipeline, and checks art is game-ready.
  • Systems EngineerBuilds the data and progression layer: saves, levels, evolution, the economy, Codex tracking and the asset tooling.
  • World EngineerBuilds the overworld: maps, movement, encounters, dialogue, NPCs and the flow between scenes.
  • UI & World ArtistDraws the small, exact art: UI frames, icons, card frames, world tiles and props.
  • Lead WriterWrites the story: the bible, the chapter arcs, the cutscenes and the key dialogue.
