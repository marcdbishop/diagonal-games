The team

Diagonal Games is run by one human founder. Everyone else on the team is an AI agent with a defined job, from battle code to art to story. The founder decides the big questions and can overrule the Director's calls. The agents do the work and check each other's.

  • DirectorKeeps the team pointed the same way and makes the calls the founder can overrule.
  • Battle EngineerBuilds the battle engine: the timeline, timed reactions, Energy, roles, enemy AI and capture.
  • Combat DesignerDesigns how battles feel and balance, so every exchange asks for a decision.
  • Creature DesignerDesigns the Pawns and their lines.
  • World DesignerDesigns the places you travel through and how they connect.
  • Narrative DesignerWrites the story and the characters in it.
  • Audio DesignerShapes the game's music and sound.
  • QA EngineerHunts for bugs and checks that fixes hold.
  • ReviewerReads the team's work before it goes in and sends back what is not ready.
  • Franklin (CCO)Talks with players and keeps the Discord and the studio's news in order.
  • Code Health EngineerKeeps the codebase tidy so the game stays easy to change.
  • PlaytesterPlays the game as a player would and reports how it feels.
  • Blackstone (CLO)Checks the studio's words and names so we do not use what is not ours.
  • Fibonacci (CFO)Looks after the studio's finances.
  • Gutenberg (CDO)Builds this website and writes the words on it.
  • UI EngineerBuilds the in-game HUD, title screen and shared UI theme, and makes them good to use.
  • Art DesignerDirects the look of the game and the art pipeline, keeping all art in the approved style.
  • Systems EngineerBuilds the data and progression layer: saves, levels, evolution, economy and the Dex.
  • World EngineerBuilds the overworld: maps, movement, encounters, dialogue and the flow between scenes.
  • UI & World ArtistDraws the small, exact art: UI frames, icons, card frames, world tiles and props.
  • Lead WriterWrite the game's story at a mature, emotional standard: the story bible, the chapter arcs, the cutscene scripts and the key dialogue, all held to the owner's tone bar.
