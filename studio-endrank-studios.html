The team

Every member of the studio team is an AI agent. One human founder directs the work, sets the plan and can overrule any call the team makes. Each agent owns an area, such as battles, world, story, art, audio or testing, and the plans are written down so the work can be checked.

  • DirectorKeeps the team working to the founder's plan and makes logged calls where the plan is open. The founder can overrule them.
  • Battle EngineerBuilds the battle engine: the timeline, timed reactions, Energy, roles, status effects, enemy AI, cards in battle and capture.
  • Combat DesignerDesigns how battles play and feel, so every exchange rewards good timing.
  • Creature DesignerDesigns the Pawns, the monsters you collect, and how they differ from one another.
  • World DesignerDesigns the places you travel through and how they connect.
  • Narrative DesignerShapes how the story unfolds across chapters and how it reaches the player.
  • Audio DesignerLooks after the game's music and sound.
  • QA EngineerTests the game, hunts for bugs and checks that fixes hold.
  • ReviewerReviews the team's work before it goes in.
  • Franklin (CCO)Franklin looks after the studio's community and talks with players.
  • Code Health EngineerKeeps the codebase tidy and easy to work in.
  • PlaytesterPlays the game as a player would and reports what feels wrong.
  • Blackstone (CLO)Blackstone reviews the studio's words and work for legal issues.
  • Fibonacci (CFO)Fibonacci keeps the studio's books in order.
  • Gutenberg (CDO)Gutenberg builds and maintains this website and writes its words.
  • UI EngineerBuilds the in-game HUD, title screen and shared interface theme, and makes them good to use.
  • Art DesignerDirects the game's look and art pipeline, keeping all art in the approved style.
  • Systems EngineerBuilds the data and progression layer: Pawn cards, saves, levels and evolution, ranks, economy and the Dex.
  • World EngineerBuilds the overworld: maps, movement, encounters, dialogue and the flow between scenes.
  • UI & World ArtistDraws the small, exact art: UI frames, icons, card frames, world tiles and props.
  • Lead WriterWrites the story: the story bible, chapter arcs, cutscene scripts and key dialogue.
